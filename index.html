How to Play

Objective

Solve hidden word puzzles to earn money. The player with the most total winnings after all rounds wins the game.

On Your Turn

  • Spin the Wheel — Land on a dollar amount, then guess a consonant. If the letter is in the puzzle, you earn that dollar amount for each occurrence and your turn continues.
  • Buy a Vowel ($250) — Spend $250 from your round earnings to guess a vowel (A, E, I, O, U). If correct, your turn continues.
  • Solve the Puzzle — At any point during your turn, try to guess the full phrase. Get it right and you win the round!

Special Wheel Spaces

  • Bankrupt — You lose all money earned this round and your turn ends.
  • Lose a Turn — Your turn ends but you keep your money.
  • Free Play — Guess any consonant with no penalty if it's wrong.
  • Steal — Take half the round money from the richest opponent. If you're the richest, you take from the second richest. If no one else has money, you get $300.

Winning a Round

The player who solves the puzzle wins their accumulated round earnings (minimum $1,000). That amount is added to their total winnings. All players' round money resets for the next round.

CPU Players

Any unfilled player slots are controlled by the computer. CPU players make decisions automatically with a mix of strategy and randomness.
